# Bundle budgets

`.size-limit.json` is the enforced version of ENGINEERING.md §11.

The budget is on **installed bytes**, not repository size: a component library's
value proposition is what a customer ships to their users, and everything else
is our problem rather than theirs.

Limits sit just above today's measured size so they **ratchet rather than
aspire**. When a number here goes up, that is a decision someone made in a pull
request — which is the point. Lower them when you can; never raise one to make
a build pass.

React and react-dom are excluded from the React measurements because they are
peer dependencies the host already has.

## The barrel moved from 9 KB to 30 KB across the Switch and Consult releases

Recorded here because the rule above says a raised number should be a decision
somebody made in a pull request, not a build turning green.

Every entry before these two measured a **loader** — a few hundred lines of SVG
and timing. Switch and Consult are a different weight class: Consult is the
first `pattern`-layer component and carries an engine (session state machine,
safety pipeline, mode scope contracts, FHIR audit mapping), measuring 21.5 KB
alone; Switch measures 13 KB alone. Deduplicated they land at 29.33 KB, so the
growth is those two components rather than drift in the loaders.

What keeps this honest rather than a blank cheque is that **the per-component
entries are the ones that matter.** The barrel is an install-everything
convenience almost nobody imports; what a customer ships is one component, and
`react — consult alone` and `react — Switch alone` both ratchet just above their
measured size like every other line here.

Worth saying plainly: a single barrel number has stopped modelling "what a small
consumer ships" now that two components dominate it. Splitting it per layer —
or dropping it in favour of the per-component entries — would say more than one
number can.
